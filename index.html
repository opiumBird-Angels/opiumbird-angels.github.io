Our Mission

Technology should serve humanity.

Opium Bird is a humanitarian project concept using the Stellar network as a transparent digital infrastructure for international participation, awareness and charitable fundraising.

Our purpose is centered around one principle: every child deserves a chance.

The initiative is designed to support humanitarian efforts connected with children affected by serious and life-changing diseases.

Stellar technology provides a public and transparent transaction environment that can make digital fundraising easier to observe and participate in.

Opium Bird does not diagnose, prescribe treatment or replace medical professionals. Medical decisions must always be made by qualified healthcare providers.

Global Health

The childhood cancer challenge.

Childhood cancer is a major global health challenge. The availability and quality of diagnosis and treatment can dramatically influence a child's chance of survival.

400K
Estimated children and adolescents aged 0–19 who develop cancer each year worldwide.
80%+
More than 80% of children with cancer can be cured in many high-income settings where comprehensive care is available.
<30%
In many low- and middle-income countries, fewer than 30% of children with cancer are cured.
Medical Knowledge

When treatment becomes a matter of access.

The objective of medical humanitarian work is not simply to raise awareness. It is to help reduce barriers that prevent children from receiving timely and appropriate care.

Childhood Cancer

Childhood cancers include several different diseases. Among the most common are leukaemias, brain tumours and certain solid tumours.

  • Leukaemias
  • Brain and central nervous system tumours
  • Lymphomas
  • Bone cancers
  • Neuroblastoma and Wilms tumour

The Effects of Cancer

Cancer and its treatment can affect a child's physical health, development and emotional wellbeing.

  • Fatigue and weakness
  • Pain and other disease-related symptoms
  • Effects of chemotherapy or radiotherapy
  • Nutritional and developmental challenges
  • Psychological and social impact on children and families
Medical Information

Plague

Plague is an infectious disease caused by the bacterium Yersinia pestis.


The most common clinical form is bubonic plague. Severe forms include pneumonic and septicemic plague.


Transmission can occur through infected fleas, direct contact with infectious material, and inhalation of respiratory particles in pneumonic plague.


Modern antibiotics are effective against plague when treatment begins promptly. Early diagnosis and medical treatment are therefore critical.


This section is provided for educational purposes and is not medical advice.
